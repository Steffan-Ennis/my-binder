Logo Exploration

Circular emblem-style logos for the binder cover screen. Each concept uses a mythical/heroic motif that works as a silver emboss on the cobalt cover, and as a standalone mark at small sizes.

A. Coiled Dragon

Serpentine dragon coiled within the circle. Bat-like wings spread to the edges. Evokes power, rarity, and the fantasy genre of collectible cards.

Mythical Dynamic Bold

B. Guardian Angel

Winged angel figure holding a card. Feathered wings spread wide within the circle. Conveys protection and curation of the collection.

Protective Elegant Iconic

C. Shield & Crest

Heraldic shield with crossed swords and a knight's helm above. Classic coat-of-arms style. Speaks to collecting as an honourable pursuit.

Heraldic Classic Sturdy

D. Rising Phoenix

Phoenix rising with wings spread wide, tail feathers trailing below. Symbolises rebirth and the enduring value of a curated collection.

Majestic Aspirational Symmetrical

E. The Collector

Hooded mage figure with arms outstretched, cards floating in an arcane arc. The collector as protagonist. Mysterious, personal, magical.

Mysterious Personal Narrative

F. Dragon & Card

Dragon coiled around a single card, claws gripping the edges. Combines the dragon motif with the core product (the card itself). The most literal mark.

Iconic Product-centric Detailed
