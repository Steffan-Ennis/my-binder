Logo Variations

Mythical creature medallion style — inspired by the dragon life counter

Reference

Original inspiration: leather-embossed twin dragon life counter. Dark medallion, coiled dragons in yin-yang arrangement, raised relief texture.

Original Leather Monochrome

A. Twin Dragons

Maroon and slate dragon silhouettes in yin-yang arrangement. Bold filled shapes, dark palette with subtle warm/cool split. Two brass-style rivets at centre.

Maroon + Slate Yin-Yang Silhouette

B. Ouroboros

Dark dragon forming a thick crescent ring, biting its own tail. Filled silhouette with spine ridges. "MY BINDER" text in the centre void.

Dark Plum Circular Silhouette

C. Coiled Spiral

Single purple dragon coiled in a tight spiral filling the entire medallion. Fire breath at the top, wings spreading wide. Bold and dense — closest to the reference's filled composition.

Purple Spiral Dense

D. Dragon & Card

Crimson dragon coiled around a central card, claws gripping the edges. Wings spread wide. Ties the dragon motif directly to the product — a guardian of the collection.

Crimson Card-centric Guardian
